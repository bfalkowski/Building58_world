# Glossary

- **Scene**: one place in 3D, built by one script from an empty file.
- **Kit**: lights, materials, camera rigs and render settings that two or more scenes use.
- **Render**: an image of a scene from one camera. Renders go to `out/` and stay out of git.
- **Smoke render**: a small, fast render that shows bpy works on this computer.
- **Builder**: a kit function that adds one shape, material or light in one call (`kit/build.py`).
- **Haze**: a thin fog in the air of a scene, so beams of light show.
- **Figure**: a plain stand-in for a person: legs, body, head and maybe a hat. No face and no likeness.
- **Group**: an empty that holds a figure or a chair, so a viewer can move it as one thing.
- **Marker**: a named point with nothing to draw, such as a seat or a place to stand.
- **Add-on**: a module from this repo that the viewer loads with `&addon=`, to change the scene as it runs.
- **Replay**: playing a night's public records in a scene, half hour by half hour.
